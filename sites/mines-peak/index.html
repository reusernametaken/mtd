Mines Peak

Candidate

Mines Peak is the high ground the whole route turns on. It sits right at the Continental Divide, where a radio tower already stands. Hooking onto existing tower infrastructure is the plan here. That's far simpler than raising something new on a bare summit.

Where it stands

Access to the tower isn't settled. I'm still waiting to hear back from several people about tower access along the corridor, and Mines is one I very much hope to get. Nothing is agreed, and I'll post here when that changes.

What a node here would do

A repeater at Mines would be the link between the Grand County side and the Front Range side. It would carry messages across the Divide, so a text sent from a companion radio in the Fraser Valley can keep going east.

Built to look after itself

The flagship node for this site is being engineered to take care of itself: solar panels, a cold-tolerant battery, and a sealed, insulated enclosure, with no wiring into anyone's building or equipment. All it needs from a tower is a place to mount.

If you can help

If you know who manages that tower, or any of the radio sites around the Divide, I'd like to hear from you. Reach out. Back to the map.

Node Builds

Mines Peak is the big question mark right now -- high enough up to actually matter for a Front Range link, but there's no easy grid power at that elevation, so a real build up there likely means wind, not just solar, to get through the dark months. Still an open question, not a solved one.

An RF engineer spoke up on Discord a while back about what a proper "mountain backbone" build actually takes -- tower siting, antenna selection, the kind of detail that turns a hopeful pin on a map into a working repeater. That's exactly where I'm hoping to rally a team for the effort.

Several known unknowns remain. Several roles need occupying. Several project facets need to actually move. That's what it's going to take for that divide to ever get meshed.

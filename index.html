Who's Building This

This is a volunteer effort, not a company. It started with one person in Kremmling deciding a community-owned mesh network was worth building even if it began as a single node on a single rooftop -- and it's grown exactly as far as neighbors, hikers, and other mesh-curious people have been willing to pitch in a radio, a mounting point, or an afternoon.

No investors, no ad revenue, no data being sold anywhere. If you've got a rooftop with a sightline west toward the Front Range, or you're already running MeshCore gear somewhere in Grand County, you're already part of who's building this.

Host a Repeater

For businesses and landowners willing to host a node. You're not donating money — you're donating something better: a home for the node. Your property becomes public infrastructure.

Host a repeater and you get permanent visibility on the mesh map itself — wherever people look up that node, they see who hosts it. If it's sitting at a place of business, that's your name and your location, seen every time someone checks coverage nearby. It's yours to build on, too: hang companion radio giveaways or event promotions on it, whatever fits — lean on the fact that you host a repeater and make meshaging viable.

We're also reserving banner space right here on mtd.2203.fyi to call out where the reliable repeaters actually are — which is to say, where the coverage is sustained. Host a node, and that's where your name goes.

Founding Location Partners get their name on the enclosure itself, a line on the community poster, and the ceremonial first ping when their node goes live.

The hardware market for this stuff moves fast — new boards, new radios, new price points showing up practically every week. We're not pretending to have a fixed, final list. Expect this page to become a running set of reviews instead: as many of the available companion and repeater options as we can actually get hands-on with and assess, updated as the landscape moves.

One thing before you buy: a companion radio only works once you're already in range of a repeater — it has nothing to talk to on its own. If there's no node near you yet, a companion device is a paperweight until one goes up.

That's exactly why the DEV/OPS: section exists. If you want to be in range sooner rather than later, that's where the repeater builds, siting, and route planning live — come help put a node where you need one.

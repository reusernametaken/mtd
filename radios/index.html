Find Radios

The hardware market for this stuff moves fast — new boards, new radios, new price points showing up practically every week. There's no fixed, final list here. Expect this page to become a running set of reviews instead: as many of the available companion and repeater options as can actually be put hands-on and assessed, updated as the landscape moves.

One thing before you buy: a companion radio only works once you're in range of a repeater — it has nothing to talk to on its own. If there's no node near you yet, a companion device is a paperweight until one goes up. Lousy paperweights too, these things — quite light, really.

What they're good for

  • Texting with no cell service. A companion radio pairs with your phone and carries short text messages over the mesh.
  • The back pasture and the backcountry. Places where the signal stops at the valley floor are where it earns its keep, once a repeater covers them.
  • A backup line. When the highway closes or a creek floods, the mesh doesn't depend on cell towers, fiber or the power grid.
  • No subscription. No monthly fee. You buy the radio once.

Want to be in range sooner? See where the chain is headed on the map, or host a repeater near you.
